Let’s clear things up

Nutritional Misinformation

In the past few decades, lobby groups vilified dairy products due to concerns over saturated fat content, which has been inaccurately linked to heart disease. This gave birth to a generation of low-fat and fat-free dairy products.

Lactose intolerance affects many people, causing digestive issues like bloating and discomfort, although we believe the type of milk may be a factor here. 

Hormones and antibiotics have long been used to ward off disease in factory farmed cows, and increase their milk production. However, the presence of hormones and antibiotics in commercially produced dairy also raises health concerns.

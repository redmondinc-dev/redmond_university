HOW TO PLAY

Two ways to build hydration.

  1. Choose Re-Lyte or the mineral challenge.
  2. Pick a flavor if you chose Re-Lyte, then open the bottle.
  3. Add cold water and either one scoop or all four minerals.
  4. Seal the lid and shake five times.

Compare the routes: both build the same electrolyte profile, but Re-Lyte puts the key minerals together in one simple scoop.
